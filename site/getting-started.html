Getting started with AI tools

Welcome to AI for Holiday Planning. This chapter helps you pick a free AI tool, write your first prompt, and keep your personal information private.

You do not need to be technical. You do not need to pay for anything. You just need a phone, tablet, or computer.

Free AI chat tools you can use today

These are popular free options. You only need an email address to sign up for most of them:

  • Google Gemini — free tier, good at lists, plans, and summaries
  • Anthropic Claude — free tier, writes in a warm, natural tone
  • OpenAI ChatGPT — free tier, good at brainstorming and step-by-step plans
  • Microsoft Copilot — free with a Microsoft account, can search the web

Pick one and stick with it while you work through this manual. You can switch later; the prompts in this guide work in any of them.

Your first prompt: a worked example

Say you want gift ideas for a coworker. Instead of "gift ideas," try something specific:

"I need gift ideas for a coworker who likes tea and puzzles. Budget: $15–$25. Please suggest 5 small, thoughtful gifts I can buy online or at a drugstore, with a one-sentence reason for each."

Notice the ingredients:

  • Who the gift is for
  • What they like
  • Your budget
  • How many ideas you want
  • Where you want to shop

That is really all there is to prompting: tell the AI who, what, how much, and how many.

Refining the results

If the first list is not great, ask for changes. You are in charge:

  • "Make them cheaper — under $15 each."
  • "One of these is not right. Give me 3 more options."
  • "Rewrite this as a shopping list I can screenshot."

Privacy reminders (read before you paste)

AI chats are not private diaries. Follow these rules every time:

  • Never paste real names. Use [Friend's Name], [Mom], or [My Coworker] instead.
  • Never paste addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses.
  • Never paste credit card numbers, bank account numbers, or passwords.
  • Never paste health details about yourself or anyone else. This guide is education only — never medical advice.

If you need to work with real details, fill them in yourself after the AI gives you a template.

Low-stress ground rules

  • One thing at a time. Do not try to plan the whole holiday season in one sitting.
  • AI makes mistakes. Double-check dates, prices, store hours, and travel details before you book or buy.
  • You decide. If an AI suggestion stresses you out, ignore it. A gentle holiday is better than a "perfect" one.
  • Rest is productive. If you are tired, stop and come back later. This manual will still be here.
