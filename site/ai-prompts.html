Copy-and-paste AI prompts

Copy any prompt below into a free AI chat tool, fill in the bracketed details, and send it. You can use these in Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, OpenAI ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, or any other free AI chat tool.

Remember: replace bracketed placeholders like [Name] with your own details. Never paste real names, addresses, phone numbers, or payment details into a public AI chat.

Gift ideas and budgets

Holiday gift budget

"Help me make a holiday gift budget. Total budget: $[amount]. People to buy for: [list]. Suggest a dollar amount for each person and leave some wiggle room for wrapping and cards. Show it as a simple table."

Gift brainstorming

"I need gift ideas for [person]. They like [interests]. My budget is about $[amount] per person. Please suggest 5–7 realistic gifts I can find online or at common stores, with a one-sentence reason for each and an estimated price. Keep it practical and low-stress."

Low-cost gift ideas

"Give me 8 free or nearly free gift ideas for [person] who [likes/interests]. Focus on thoughtful, personal, low-cost gifts."

Overspending check

"I have already spent $[amount] on gifts out of my $[total] budget. I still need gifts for [list]. Help me adjust my plan so I stay within budget."

Travel plans

Trip itinerary

"Help me plan a [number]-day trip to [destination], leaving [date] and returning [date]. We are [driving/flying/taking the train] with [who]. Please make a simple day-by-day itinerary with travel times, one or two activities per day, rest time built in, and meal suggestions. Keep it low-stress — no more than two big activities per day. Budget is about $[amount] per day for food and activities."

Packing checklist

"Make a packing list for a [number]-day trip to [destination] in [month], for [who: e.g., two adults and one toddler]. Include clothes, toiletries, medicines, chargers, travel documents, kid items, and anything easy to forget. Organize it as a checklist with [ ] boxes."

Travel budget

"Help me estimate a travel budget for a trip to [destination], [number] days, [number] people, traveling by [car/plane/train]. Include categories: transport, lodging, food, activities, and an emergency buffer of 10%. Show it as a table with estimated costs."

Delayed plans

"My [flight/train] is delayed and I will arrive at [time] instead of [time]. Help me re-plan my first evening simply, with rest built in."

Gathering checklists

Invitation

"Help me draft a friendly invitation for a [type of gathering] on [date] at [time] at [location]. Include: what to bring, RSVP deadline, parking details, and whether kids or pets are welcome. Keep the tone warm and low-pressure."

Prep timeline

"I am hosting a [type of gathering] for [number] people on [date] at [time]. Make a prep timeline working backward from the event: one week before, a few days before, the day before, the morning of, and 2 hours before guests arrive. Include shopping, cooking, cleaning, and setting the table. Keep it realistic for one person with low energy."

Day-of run sheet

"Make a simple hour-by-hour run sheet for a [type of gathering] starting at [time]. Include: guest arrival, food serving, and winding down. Keep it flexible."

Dietary accommodations

"I am cooking for [number] people. One person is [vegetarian/gluten-free/dairy-free/nut-free/diabetic]. Suggest [number] dishes that work for everyone without making separate meals."

Meal plans

Meal ideas from what you have

"I have these ingredients: [list]. Suggest 5 simple meals I can make using mostly these ingredients, plus a few common extras. Include prep time and cook time for each."

Multi-day meal plan

"Plan [number] days of meals for [number] people around [holiday]. Include breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Keep recipes simple, with minimal chopping. Note which meals can be made ahead or reheated."

Grocery list

"Turn this meal plan into an organized grocery list grouped by store section (produce, dairy, meat, pantry, frozen): [paste your meal plan]. Include quantities for [number] people."

Cooking timeline

"I want to eat dinner at [time]. Here is my menu: [list dishes]. Make a cooking timeline starting [number] hours before dinner. Keep it simple and mark what can be done the day before."

Leftovers

"I have leftover [food]. Give me 4 simple meals to use them up within 3–4 days, with storage and reheating tips."

Post-holiday decluttering

Decluttering decision help

"I am decluttering after the holidays. I have [item] and I cannot decide whether to keep it or donate it. Ask me 3 questions to help me decide, one at a time."

Leftover food plan

"I have leftover [food]. Give me 4 simple meals to use them up within 3–4 days, with storage and reheating tips."

Donation plan

"I have a box of items to donate: [types of items, e.g., clothes, toys, unopened food]. Suggest where I could donate them locally and how to prepare the box."

Using the prompts well

  1. Copy the prompt into your AI chat tool.
  2. Fill in the brackets with your real details.
  3. Read the AI's answer and keep what helps, ignore what does not.
  4. Ask follow-ups if something is unclear: "Can you make that simpler?" or "Give me 3 more options."
  5. Copy the final result into a planner page, your notes app, or print it from site/planners/.
