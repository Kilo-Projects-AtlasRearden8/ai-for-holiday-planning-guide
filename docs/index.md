Use AI to take the stress out of the holidays: gift ideas and budgets, travel plans, gathering checklists, meal plans, and post-holiday decluttering.

## Who this is for

You do not need to be technical. You do not need to pay for AI. You just need a holiday season ahead of you and about thirty minutes to set things up.

This manual is written for anyone who wants a kinder, lower-stress holiday — parents, caregivers, nurses and healthcare workers, shift workers, hosts, travelers, gift-givers, and anyone who has ever felt overwhelmed by a to-do list.

It assumes you have never used AI before, or have only played with it a little. Every step is spelled out in plain English.

## Our approach: kind and low-stress

The holidays do not have to be perfect. They just have to work for you and the people you care about. This manual is built around a few ideas:

- **AI is a helper, not a boss.** You always review and decide. If an AI suggestion does not feel right, skip it.
- **Done is better than perfect.** A simple plan you actually use beats a fancy plan you abandon.
- **Low energy is okay.** If you are tired, working shifts, or managing ADHD, pick one small thing at a time. Rest is part of the plan.
- **Privacy first.** Never paste real names, addresses, phone numbers, or financial account numbers into a public AI chat. Use placeholders like `[Friend's Name]` or `[Street Address]` instead.

## What AI can (and cannot) do for your holidays

**AI can help with:**

- Brainstorming gift ideas within a budget
- Building gift and spending trackers
- Drafting travel itineraries and packing lists
- Making gathering checklists and day-of run sheets
- Planning meals and grocery lists from what you already have
- Breaking post-holiday decluttering into small, manageable steps
- Turning a messy brain dump into a clean plan

**AI cannot:**

- Buy gifts or book travel for you
- Know your family traditions, dietary needs, or local rules
- Guarantee anything will go smoothly
- Replace your judgment — you always review and decide

## How to use this manual

1. Pick the section you need right now (gifts, travel, gathering, meals, or decluttering).
2. Work through the steps in order.
3. Copy the AI prompts into a free AI chat tool, fill in your details, and paste the results into the printable planners.
4. Print the planners in `site/planners/` or fill them out on your phone or computer.
5. Check things off as you go. Skip anything that does not apply to you.
